Profile

I am a statistical geneticist working at the intersection of human genetics, population genomics, and research platform development. My background spans statistics, computer science, and biomedical informatics, with experience across academic research and biotechnology.

At DKFZ/GHGA, I coordinate the technical architecture and scientific use of the Trusted Research Environment supporting the genomic pillar of the German National Cohort (NAKO). I completed my Dr. rer. nat. at the School of Medicine and Health of the Technical University of Munich (TUM) while based at Helmholtz Pioneer Campus, investigating tissue-specific mitochondrial–nuclear regulation. The common thread in my work is making complex genomic data actionable through rigorous analysis, reproducible workflows, and interoperable research infrastructure.

Scientific Poster, genomDE Symposium, Bonn, September 2026

TREND: A trusted research environment for NAKO Genomics Data.

We present TREND, a browser-based Trusted Research Environment that enables secure analysis of German National Cohort (NAKO) data without giving researchers downloadable copies of, or direct file-system access to, raw participant-level data. TREND combines encrypted, pseudonymized data storage, job-based high-performance computing, and a tamper-evident audit trail, while data-access decisions remain with the NAKO governance body. An end-to-end GWAS workflow using regenie and PLINK ran successfully on NAKO-scale data comprising 200,000 participants and 15 phenotypes, demonstrating the technical feasibility of standard statistical-genetics workflows in the environment.

Trusted Research Environments, NAKO, GHGA, GWAS, High-Performance Computing, Data Governance

Preprint, bioRxiv, 2024

Interplay between mitochondrial and nuclear DNA in gene expression regulation.

By using linear mixed models (LMMs) to conduct expression quantitative trait locus (eQTL) mapping, we explored the crosstalk between mitochondria and the cell nucleus across 48 different tissues. Through the integration of our identified eQTLs with various molecular data, we applied a series of analyses, including statistical colocalization, causal inference, graph neural networks, and over-representation analysis. These methods helped us prioritize candidate disease-associated genes, variants, and ontological pathways. Our study shows potential for unveiling new therapeutic approaches aimed at a wide range of diseases, from metabolic disorders to neurodegenerative conditions.

Mito-Nuclear Interactions, Mediation, Colocalization, Mendelian Randomization, Graph Neural Networks, Interpretable Machine Learning

Preprint, bioRxiv, 2024

Tissue-specific apparent mtDNA heteroplasmy and its relationship with ageing and mtDNA gene expression.

In this study, we comprehensively characterize the landscape of common mtDNA heteroplasmy and mtRNA modifications across 49 human tissues assayed with bulk RNA-seq in GTEx v8. As mtDNA heteroplasmy and mtRNA modifications are tissue-specific, we aim to establish a robust framework for identifying and testing both types of variation from tissue-specific RNA-seq data. Our study provides the first comprehensive and tissue-specific description of the apparent mtDNA heteroplasmy, their relationship with donor age, and their regulatory effects on mitochondrial gene expression.

Mito-Nuclear Interactions, Interpretable Machine Learning, Gene Expression Regulation, Heteroplasmy

Master Thesis, Thessaly University 2020

Quality Control and Imputation of Genotype data from different Illumina arrays

This thesis explores the importance of quality control (QC) procedures in genome-wide association studies (GWAS), which are vital for ensuring the trustworthiness of genetic associations. It focuses on integrating cohorts from different array versions, tackling the computational hurdles and genetic assumptions present in Illumina genotyping data. By employing thorough QC filtering and imputation methods, the thesis introduces a robust framework for detecting outlier individuals and SNPs, ultimately generating a quality-controlled dataset suitable for precise association testing.

QC, GWAS, Osteoarthritis, Imputation, Genomic Variation, Bioinformatics, Statistical Genetics, PCA
