About

My work sits at the intersection of statistical genetics, data infrastructure, and biomedical AI. After a PhD and postdoctoral research at Helmholtz Munich and TUM, focused on mitonuclear regulation and Alzheimer's disease multi-omics, I moved into industry to build the genomic evidence systems that drug discovery teams actually need.

At Pheiron I led the architecture of biobank-scale GWAS and eQTL integration pipelines — harmonizing FinnGen, UK Biobank, and MVP across 36M+ target-indication pairs. I am now joining GHGA at DKFZ Heidelberg to work on federated genomic data infrastructure, access governance, and interoperability standards for the German Human Genome-Phenome Archive.

I write about the limits of statistical inference in biology, genomic data governance, and what it means to build infrastructure for science rather than just for publication.
